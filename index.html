How it works

Three steps to your first tailored CV.

Install CViper Light

From the Microsoft Store or the installer above. It opens straight into a short welcome.

Paste your OpenAI key

The welcome screen links to where you get one and shows what a typical CV costs. One paste, done.

Drop in your CV and a job

Paste the job advert or its link. You get a match score, a tailored CV and a cover letter you can edit and save.

Questions

Before you download.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free and there is no paid tier. The only cost is what OpenAI charges for the AI calls you make with your own key — typically a few pence for a tailored CV.

Where do I get an OpenAI key, and what does it cost?

From your OpenAI account under "API keys". You add a small amount of credit and pay only for what you use. The app's welcome screen links straight there and shows a cost estimate before each run.

Is my CV safe?

It stays on your computer. When you ask for a tailored version, the text goes from your PC directly to OpenAI using your key, under OpenAI's API terms — not through us. We never see it.

Windows only?

Yes, for now. A macOS build exists but is not publicly signed, so we do not offer it. If enough people ask, that changes.

Does it make things up?

It is designed not to. It rewrites what is in your CV to match the role; it does not add skills or jobs you did not give it. Always read the result before you send it.

Who built this?

One person — a product manager with years in London banking and finance hiring — as a side project. It is maintained in spare time; questions and bugs go through GitHub Issues.

Privacy, in one paragraph. CViper Light runs on your computer. We have no server, no accounts, and no copy of your data. Your OpenAI key and your CV never leave your machine except to reach OpenAI directly. This website sets no cookies.
